Glinto Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026

Glinto is a trading-card portfolio tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. The short version: we store the collection data you create, we don't run ads, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone.

Data you give us

  • Account: if you create an account, we store your email address (or the identifier provided by Sign in with Apple / Google) to authenticate you and sync your data across devices. You can also use the app as a guest without an account.
  • Collection data: the cards you add — including purchase prices, purchase dates, conditions, grades, sale records — plus your watchlist and its price targets. This is the core of the app and is stored so your portfolio syncs and persists.

Data we generate

  • Portfolio history: daily snapshots of your portfolio's value, used to draw your performance charts.

Camera

The card scanner uses your camera to read a card's name and number. All recognition happens on your device. Photos are never uploaded, stored, or shared — they are discarded immediately after the text is read.

Notifications

If you turn on price alerts, we store an Apple push notification token for your device so we can tell you when a card on your watchlist hits your target price. The token identifies the device, not you, and is used only to deliver those alerts. Turning notifications off in iOS Settings stops them, and deleting your account removes the token. We never send marketing notifications.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising SDKs.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your personal data.
  • No collection of your location, contacts, or photos.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Where data lives

Your data is stored with Supabase (hosted on Amazon Web Services, US region), protected by row-level security so only your authenticated account can read it. Market pricing data shown in the app is aggregate public sales data and is not tied to you.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app: Settings → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible — your holdings, watchlist, history, and account record are removed from our servers.

Children

Glinto is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
