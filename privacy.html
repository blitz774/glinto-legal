Glinto Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Glinto is a trading-card portfolio tracker operated by Bilal Afzal ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Glinto iOS app and the website glintoapp.com, and explains what data they handle and how. The short version: we store the collection data you create, we don't run ads, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone.

Data you give us

  • Account: if you create an account, we store your email address (or the identifier provided by Sign in with Apple / Google) to authenticate you and sync your data across devices. You can also use the app as a guest without an account.
  • Collection data: the cards you add — including purchase prices, purchase dates, conditions, grades, sale records — plus your watchlist and its price targets. This is the core of the app and is stored so your portfolio syncs and persists.

Data we generate

  • Portfolio history: daily snapshots of your portfolio's value, used to draw your performance charts.
  • Scan count: how many cards you have scanned this month, so the free plan's monthly scan limit can be applied. It is a number per month, not a record of what you scanned.

Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to Sentry, our error-monitoring provider: the app version, device model, iOS version, and a technical trace of what failed. A small sample of sessions (about 1 in 10) also sends performance timings, such as how long a screen or request took. These reports contain no account details or collection data and are not linked to you. We use them only to find and fix bugs.

Camera

The card scanner uses your camera to read a card's name and number. All recognition happens on your device. Photos are never uploaded, stored, or shared — they are discarded immediately after the text is read.

Notifications

If you turn on price alerts, we store an Apple push notification token for your device so we can tell you when a card on your watchlist hits your target price. The token identifies the device, not you, and is used only to deliver those alerts. Turning notifications off in iOS Settings stops them, and deleting your account removes the token. We never send marketing notifications.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising SDKs.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. The only third-party SDKs in the app are Supabase (our database) and Sentry (crash reports, above).
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your personal data.
  • No collection of your location, contacts, or photos.

Purchases

Glinto Pro subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app asks Apple whether your subscription is active; we don't keep purchase records on our servers.

Links to TCGplayer and eBay

Glinto links to TCGplayer and eBay so you can buy a card or look it up. Some of these links may be affiliate links: if you buy something after following one, we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. When you follow a link you leave Glinto, and that store's own privacy policy applies; it may use cookies to credit the referral. We don't send stores your account or collection data.

The website

glintoapp.com is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs such as IP addresses. The homepage loads live market figures from our database (Supabase) and card images from TCGplayer's image servers; those requests carry nothing about you beyond what any web request does. The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Where data lives

Your data is stored with Supabase (hosted on Amazon Web Services, US region), protected by row-level security so only your authenticated account can read it. Crash reports are processed by Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) in the US. Market pricing data shown in the app is aggregate public sales data and is not tied to you.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app: Settings → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible — your holdings, watchlist, history, and account record are removed from our servers.

Children

Glinto is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
