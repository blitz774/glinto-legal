Prices you can check.

Raw prices by printing and condition from TCGplayer market data, refreshed several times a day. Graded prices by grader and grade, where slabs trade.

  • Flagged, not guessed. When a card's own listings contradict its market price, Glinto says so instead of picking a number.
  • Gaps stay gaps. Each price is plotted on the date it changed, with more than a year of history on most cards worth $25 or more. Missing stretches are marked, never filled in.
  • No number, no pretending. When there's no price for a card or a grade, the app tells you.

Know what's moving.

Search about 290,000 cards and 2,000 sealed products. See the week's biggest movers and where they moved from, market indices by game, and a weekly recap.

Point the camera at a card and Glinto reads its name and number on your phone. The photo never leaves it.

The Lab.

Rip Lab works out what a pack is expected to be worth against what it costs today, from real pull rates and current single prices, for the sets where pull rates are known. Most packs lose money, and it shows you by how much.

Market Pulse shows how many cards are up versus down in each game, the most volatile cards, and where grading pays.

Your collection, in detail.

Add raw copies and graded slabs, record what you paid, and see profit and loss per card and across your collection. Mark cards as sold to track what you actually made.

Set a price to buy below or sell above, and Glinto alerts you when it's hit.
